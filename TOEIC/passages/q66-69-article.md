## 单词/短语讲解

1. **The Psychology of Shopping is the latest documentary by Sandler Jenkins, the award-winning director.**
   - documentary /ˌdɑːkjəˈmentəri/：纪录片
   - award-winning /əˈwɔːrd ˌwɪnɪŋ/：获过奖的
   - director /dəˈrektər/：这里是电影导演（下一篇信里的 director 是分店负责人）

2. **The movie, which was released on Friday, explains the tricks that store owners use to encourage shoppers to buy at their establishments.**（66 空填 was released）
   - be released /rɪˈliːst/：（电影）上映、公开发行
   - tricks：手段、小花招
   - encourage A to do：促使、引导 A 去做
   - establishment /ɪˈstæblɪʃmənt/：店铺、营业场所，不要译成"建立"

3. **The $5,000,000 generated by the film in its first weekend is not quite a record, but this is still very high revenue for a documentary.**
   - generate /ˈdʒenəreɪt/：带来、创造（收入）
   - not quite a record：还算不上纪录，否定句里 quite 表示"还差一点"
   - revenue /ˈrevənuː/：收入，这里指票房收入
   - for a documentary：就纪录片而言

4. **It is easy to see why it has been this ---67---.**
   - It is easy to see why…：不难理解为什么……
   - this + 形容词：这么……

5. **The subject is fascinating and the movie provides many insights into the retail industry.**
   - subject：题材、主题（不是"主语/科目"）
   - fascinating /ˈfæsɪneɪtɪŋ/：非常吸引人的
   - provide insights into /ˈɪnsaɪts/：让人深入了解……
   - retail industry /ˈriːteɪl/：零售业

6. **---68---, supermarkets purposefully place staple items such as bread and milk at the back of the building.**
   - purposefully /ˈpɜːrpəsfəli/：有意地、故意地
   - staple items /ˈsteɪpəl/：日常必需品、主要食品
   - at the back of：在……的最里面/后部

7. **---69---.**（原文这句是：Every shopper must walk past most items in the store to reach them.）
   - walk past：从……旁边走过
   - reach：走到、够到

8. **The movie is being shown at all major theaters nationwide through the end of November.**
   - is being shown：正在上映（被动进行时）
   - major theaters：各大影院
   - nationwide /ˌneɪʃənˈwaɪd/：在全国范围
   - through the end of November：到 11 月底（美式 through 含当天）
