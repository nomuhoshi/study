## Q36-38 会议发言节选

W: Now that everyone has arrived, let's begin our meeting. I'm excited to share that our membership enrollment numbers have increased since we finished installing the new workout machines. However, our older members are having difficulty learning how to use them. If you notice someone having trouble using a machine, be sure to help them out. There's a QR code on each machine that links to an instructional video. You can help by showing them how to access the video.

app 词汇：enrollment = 登録、install = ~を設置する、workout = 運動、instructional = 説明の、facility = 施設、regulation = 規則

## Q39-41 演讲

Friends and colleagues: it's difficult to describe the emotions I feel right now. My career at Barrow Manufacturing has been such a privilege that no further reward seems necessary. Still, it's very gratifying to be presented with this tremendous honor. As Employee of the Year has traditionally gone to one of our sales associates, I'm proud to be the first employee from public relations ever to receive this prestigious title. I'd like to extend special thanks to my department supervisor, Mr. Pilani, for nominating me, and to everyone throughout the firm for your support.

app 提示：
- nominate = ~を指名する、推薦する
- extend = 本义是「(物を)伸ばす、(期間などを)延長する」，也有「(気持ちなどを)伝える」的意思；extend (one's) thanks [appreciation] = 表示感谢；extend an invitation = 发出邀请。TOEIC 里频出
- such a ... that ~ = 如此……以至于~（页面下方有这条注释，后面的内容被遮住了）

## Q48-50 电话留言 + 招牌图

图表：四种店铺招牌样式 Style 1 / Style 2 / Style 3 / Style 4（图本身不存，只是四种招牌的外观示意，每个都写着 Store Sign）

W: Good evening. This is Debra Sanders. I own a cosmetics store in Florida. We're planning a grand reopening event in three months, and I was hoping to purchase a new custom sign for the front of the store. I looked over your popular styles, and I like the one that's just letters attached directly to the storefront. However, my area gets lots of tropical storms, so I'm concerned about the sign's resistance to weather. If you could get back to me soon to discuss this, it would be greatly appreciated.

app 提示：问的是 product durability（产品的耐久性），原文说的是 resistance to weather

## Q42-44 录音留言

You have reached the Brook Street branch of Cartwright Savings & Loan in downtown Tulia. Our regular business hours are 9:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M., Monday through Friday. However, please be aware that this branch will be temporarily closed from October ninth to October eleventh. On October twelfth, we will reopen at 83 Farland Street, just one block away from our current location. This new location is larger and will enable us to serve our customers more efficiently. We will hold a re-opening reception on October fourteenth. Join us for cake, refreshments, and freebies. Thank you for calling. To replay this message, press the star key now.

app 提示：
- reception 是 receive（接待）的名词，原意是「接待」→「招待客人的场所」→「宴会、派对」。很多时候译作「欢迎会」，但不一定是欢迎会，记成「宴会、派对」即可。这里的 reception 对应题目里的 party
- 注意「晚一步」模式：To replay this message, press the star key now. 这类指示在录音最后才出现，如果只盯着选项预读，会在这句话出现之前就错过
