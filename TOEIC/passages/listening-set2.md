## Part 2（应答问题）

### Q4（直接疑问文）

When will the new equipment be installed?

(A) We did receive the installation guide.
(B) A technician will do it this afternoon.
(C) At the back of the factory.

app 词汇与提示：
- When will 连读时，will 会被说得很轻，听起来像「ウル」，容易漏听
- equipment = 装置、機器；install = 取り付ける；installation 是和 install 发音相近的干扰词
- technician = 技術者，technique（技术）+ ian（人）；TOEIC 里常考的职业，实际多指「修理机器的人」

### Q5（直接疑問文）

Which of these laptop computers has more memory?

(A) At a local electronics store.
(B) They both have the same.
(C) Yes, I bought it online.

app 提示：Which（どちら）+ more（より多くの）= 在两台电脑里问哪台内存更大

### Q6（直接疑問文）

Fran, how about discussing the revisions to the contract after lunch?

(A) To get his contact information.
(B) Since this afternoon.
(C) Do we need to do that today?

app 提示：How about doing ~?（~するのはいかがですか）是提议的表达

### Q7（付加疑問文）

You have a doctor's appointment today, don't you?

(A) No, I cancelled it.
(B) May I see your ticket?
(C) A little disappointed.

app 提示：appointment = 予約。比起「アポ（工作上的约定）」，更常用在「和医生见面 → 医院的预约」。本来是「和有相应职位的人见面」的意思

## Part 3（会话）

### Q8-10

M: This is John Greenfield from Silverdale Farms. I received an e-mail that you'd like to write an article about our farm for your Web site.

W: Yes, thank you for calling. We're very interested in your sustainable farming methods, and our readers are always looking for ways to support environmentally friendly businesses.

M: In that case, you should come down to the farm, and we'll give you a tour. You can learn a lot by speaking to our staff and owner.

W: That would be great! Is it okay if we take pictures and record some video footage of the workers in action?

M: Of course. However, some areas will be off-limits. I'll confirm the approved shoot locations with my supervisor and send you a list later.

app 提示：give you a tour 对应选项里的 Tour a property / Tour a farm；the supervisor = 上司

### Q11-13

W: Did you hear the news about the city park? They decided to build a venue for outdoor music events.

M: There's definitely enough space in the park for one.

W: And our city is already known for its strong music culture. It makes sense to try to attract more artists and music fans.

M: Yeah, that would probably boost the economy and strengthen the city's image.

W: Supposedly, there's going to be a central stage for large concerts, as well as two side stages for music festivals.

M: Oh, wow, I'm sure there will be some really cool festivals! I'll definitely be taking my family to one.

app 提示：
- 男性说 "There's definitely enough space in the park for one." 是在对女性刚说的决定表示同意（one = a venue）
- reception / festival 这类词在选项里常被概括成 an event（音乐祭 → an event）

### Q14-16（带平面图）

平面图：走廊两侧各两个房间，左侧上下是 Room B-2、Room B-1，右侧上下是 Room B-4、Room B-3，Front Desk 在下方，大门在底部。

W: Hi, welcome to Tanner Financial. How can I help you?

M: Hello. My name's Derek. I'm with Sheckter Heating and Cooling, and I'm here to fix one of your air conditioning units.

W: Oh, great. It hasn't been working since yesterday, and it's supposed to be very hot today. Let me get you a visitor's badge... What's your last name?

M: Sandberg.

W: OK... here's your visitor's badge.

M: Thank you. I'll need to access the roof of your building. Is there somebody who can show me where to do that?

W: Yes. Someone from the facility management team can do that. You'll find the department's office down the hall in the second room on the right.

app 提示：
- I'm here to ~ = 为了~而来，是说明来访目的的重要表达
- fix 被换说成 repair；one of your air conditioning units 被抽象成 a machine
- 女性问 last name，是在问姓

## Part 4（独白）

### Q17-19（会议开场）

M: OK, let's get started. Our recent surveys on brand awareness indicate that our current marketing strategies aren't effective enough. Despite our investments in social media and magazine ads, customers displayed little brand recognition for our products compared to our competitors'. Today, I'd like us to come up with some solutions. These may include new marketing methods, of course, but first I'd like us to focus on the content of our ads and brainstorm some ideas to make them more memorable. There's a lot to discuss, and the IT team needs this room at two thirty. So, let's begin.

app 词汇：
- awareness：認知
- indicate：示す
- recognition：認知
- competitor：競合他社
- brainstorm：ブレインストーミングする
- memorable：覚えやすい
- compare：比較する
- decline：～を断る

app 提示：come up with（想出）在后面的选项里被换说成 brainstorm 的意思
