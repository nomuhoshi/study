## Q18-20 会话（三人）

M: Hank, Vanessa, thank you for coming. How's the development of the new tablet coming along?

W: Well, my team's still working on the camera. It's difficult to meet the image quality and thickness requirements at the same time, but we expect to be finished within the month.

M: And my team's just completed the visual design for the operating system.

M: Sounds like it's coming along smoothly. Next, we'll have to get some working prototypes built for testing.

app 词汇：electronic = 電子の、supplier = 供給業者（其余注释截图里被遮住了）

## Q21-23 会话

W: Hi, Martin. Some gym members have mentioned that the weight training room is too hot. I think it's due to the recent humidity.

M: We have several large fans in the storage room, but we should get a few air conditioners.

W: Good idea. Have you seen the promotional flyers from that new appliance store?

M: I did, but I'm not sure where they are now. They're probably in the staff break room. I'll go take a look.

app 词汇：humidity = 湿度、promotional = 販売促進の、flyer = チラシ（后面的注释截图里被遮住了）
app 提示：appliances 是 air conditioner 的概括说法

## Q24-26 会话

W: Excuse me, Abdul. Do you have an update on the delivery of the laptops we ordered? We need them for the new hires who are starting next week.

M: Oh, thanks for reminding me. The order should arrive Wednesday afternoon, but someone will have to set up the laptops and distribute them. I'll be away at an IT conference, though. Could you handle everything when they arrive?

W: I'm sorry, but I'll be busy updating the servers that day. How about asking Oscar?

app 词汇：update = 最新情報、delivery = 配送、new hire = 新入社員、equipment = 機器、unavailable = 都合が悪い、conduct = ~を行う（其余被遮住）
app 提示：servers 被换说成 equipment

## Q27-29 会话

W: Hey, Alan. I heard you worked in Shanghai at your previous job.

M: Yeah, it was great. I was there for four years.

W: Well, there's a Shanghai restaurant near here you might like. A few of us in the department wanted to go there for lunch this weekend.

M: Oh, that place on Third Street? I heard it was really good. When were you planning to go there?

W: We were thinking about meeting there at twelve thirty on Sunday.

M: Hmm... I'm going to a business workshop on Sunday. It starts at one, so I don't think I can come. I'm free on Saturday, though.

W: Well, I'll ask the others if Saturday is okay and let you know.

app 提示：the others（同部门的几个人）被换说成 coworkers

M: Hi, Monica. It's Sheldon McCaffrey from Simotech. We're inviting our suppliers to tour our laboratory during the last week of February. I was hoping you could come and see how we're using your company's equipment.

W: I'm sorry, Sheldon. We're running a booth at a technology expo in February. I'll be in Melbourne that week.

M: I see. Well, maybe your assistant Charlie could come in your place? Or will he be in Melbourne as well?

W: No, he won't be joining us. I'll give you his extension number. I'm sure he'd love to take the tour.

app 提示：extension number（内线号码）被换说成 contact information；Melbourne 的发音要注意

## Q33-35 会话 + 天气预报

图表：天气预报

| Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunny | Rainy | Cloudy | Sunny |

M: Irene, you want to plant flowers outside the store on Wednesday, right?

W: Yes, that's right. Friday will be a national holiday. I want this store's garden to look nice before that. Why do you ask?

M: Well, look at the forecast in this newspaper. It's supposed to rain on Wednesday.

W: Oh, that's too bad. I'll have to do it one day earlier, then, since Thursday is my day off.

app 词汇与提示：be supposed to ~ = ~することになっている、~するはずだ（这里 It's supposed to rain = 据说会下雨）；day off = 休息日；national holiday = 国民の祝日
