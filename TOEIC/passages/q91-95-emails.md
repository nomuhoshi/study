## 邮件 2

To: kabirsarkar@stantonapparel.ca
From: sales@riedegerfabrics.com
Date: 12 September
Subject: Re:Flame-resistant fabrics
Attachment: Product data

Hello Mr. Sarkar,

Thank you for reaching out to us. We have some great products for general applications, as well as the specific one you asked about. I recommend cotton- or Thormex-based materials for all items and have attached a list of our most suitable products.

Cotton is of course very popular due to its light weight and breathability. We chemically treat the fabric to make it flame-resistant. However, the property will wear off after a year if washed once a week.

Thormex is a material developed by our company. It is inherently flame-resistant, and unlike most natural fibers, it repels dirt and oil. This helps maintain its flame resistance no matter how it is used.

Unfortunately, due to a materials shortage, our FR Jupiter fabric is out of stock at the moment. However, I have included it in the list because we expect to restart production in six months. You may want to consider it as long as you have no immediate need for this kind of fabric.

I look forward to hearing back from you.

Sincerely,

Ursula Fischer
Head of Sales
